Where to find things
  • Branding & Photo — your profile photo (used on Home + About) and site tagline.
  • Home / About Page — the headline text and stats on those pages.
  • Services — add, remove, or reprice any service. Changes appear on both the Home page teaser and the full Services page.
  • Portfolio — add, remove, or edit projects and upload real renders. Featured projects also appear on the Home page.
  • Awards & Achievements — shown on the About page.
  • Contact & Social — your email, WhatsApp, and social links, used across every page footer.
  • Theme — instantly recolor the entire live site.
  • Security — change your admin username and password.
Good to know: this Admin Panel saves changes to this browser's storage. Content will update instantly on the live site when viewed in this same browser. To make edits appear for every visitor on every device, the site would need a real server-based backend — ask any time if you'd like that upgrade built.
