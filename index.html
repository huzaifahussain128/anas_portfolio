What I Do

Mechanical design services, from concept to fabrication-ready

Every engagement starts with your reference — a sketch, a rough dimension set, or an existing part — and ends with a clean, manufacturable SolidWorks deliverable.

01

SolidWorks 3D Modeling

Accurate 3D parts and mechanical components built from sketches, dimensions, drawings, or reference images.

02

Sheet Metal Design

Practical sheet metal components with proper bends, thicknesses, bend allowances, flat patterns and manufacturing considerations.

03

Mechanical Assemblies

Detailed SolidWorks assemblies with multiple components, mates, configurations and proper assembly structure.

04

2D Engineering Drawings

Professional manufacturing drawings with dimensions, tolerances, material specs, section views and detailed annotations.

05

Product Design & Development

Transforming concepts and ideas into functional mechanical products and manufacturable CAD designs.

06

CAD Conversion & Modeling

Converting sketches, PDFs, images or existing references into accurate 3D CAD models and engineering drawings.
